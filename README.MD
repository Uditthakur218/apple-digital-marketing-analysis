# Apple Digital Marketing Campaign Analysis

## Project Overview
An end-to-end performance analysis of Apple's multi-channel digital marketing campaigns across YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn. The project evaluates content engagement, audience growth trends, hashtag effectiveness, and return on ad spend (ROAS) to deliver actionable marketing optimization strategies.

## Tools & Skills Used
- Tool: Microsoft Excel (Advanced Pivot Tables, Data Modeling, Lookup & Statistical Formulas, Conditional Formatting)
- Skills: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Campaign Performance Tracking, KPI Benchmarking, Content Optimization, Trend & Correlation Analysis

## Project Workflow
1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Standardized raw post metrics, campaign metadata, and weekly engagement data; handled missing values and structured date-time formats.
2. Metrics Engineering:** Calculated key marketing KPIs including Engagement Rate, Followers Growth Rate, Uplift Percentages, and Campaign ROI.
3. Exploratory Analysis:** Built multi-dimensional Pivot Tables to analyze cross-platform interaction metrics (likes, shares, comments, clicks).
4. Campaign & Content Evaluation:** Assessed engagement efficiency across content formats (Video, Carousel, Image, Tweet) and hashtag reach.
5. Insights & Recommendations:** Derived data-backed business insights for budget allocation and content distribution strategies.

## Key Findings & Business Insights
- Platform Performance: Twitter achieved the highest average engagement rate (~13.89%), followed by LinkedIn (~12.41%), while YouTube generated the highest absolute interaction volume.
- Top Content Formats: Video content drove the highest average engagement contribution (~13.93%), significantly outperforming static images and text posts.
- High-Impact Hashtags: `#AppleLifestyle` and `#AppleMusic` yielded the highest engagement rates (~13.49%), while `#AppleEvent` led in total post frequency and click reach.
- Campaign ROI: Major launch campaigns (e.g., iPhone 16 Launch, Apple Watch Ultra 2) demonstrated high engagement uplift, showing a positive correlation between targeted ad spend and net follower gains.
